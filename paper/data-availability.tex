\section*{Data-Availability Statement}\label{sec:data-availability}
The Lean 4 formalization, including the impossibility result \Cref{thm:fpd-undecidability}, is available with build instructions at \url{https://github.com/Arcadia-Y/fixing-the-fixpoint/}. The repository also provides a paper-to-Lean mapping that links the paper's definitions and results to their formal declarations. The development is described in \secref{sec:formalization}.
